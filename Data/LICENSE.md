# Data licensing status

The maintainers have not yet selected a final license for the newly contributed questions and annotations in Hero Dataset. This notice records that status; it does not grant a new data license or apply a code license to the questions, annotations, or third-party corpus.

The corpus includes material from Wikipedia and Wikidata. `source_mapping.jsonl` records the upstream project, page or entity, fixed revision, and available links for every complete source. `annotations/evidence_sources.jsonl` also records upstream identities for question-specific evidence. Existing rights and license requirements for upstream material continue to apply.

Final license text, attribution, and redistribution notices remain to be supplied by the maintainers. No CC, MIT, or other license has been selected on their behalf. Public availability of the repository is not a blanket license for its contents.
